Bingers Scrobbler

Marks what you stream as watched on Bingers. Unofficial.

1. Sign in to Bingers

Not signed in.

  1. Click Open token page, and sign in to Bingers if it asks.
  2. Under Personal tokens, click Create, and choose read + write.
  3. Copy the token (it starts with bng_pat_), paste it below, and click Save.

Needs the Bingers Legend or Icon plan. Keep the token secret: anyone who has it can change your library.

Open token page

2. When to mark as watched

Paused time and skipping ahead don't count toward the watch time. Short videos only need half their length.

4. Other sites

Watching on a site that isn't listed above? Open it, click the extension's icon, and choose Track videos on this site. The browser asks your permission for that one site, and it shows up here.
